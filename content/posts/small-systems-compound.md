---
title: Small systems compound
excerpt: A lightweight process can create more room for good work than another tool, another meeting, or another sprint.
category: Product
date: "2026-09-24"
readingTime: 4
---

Most useful systems are small enough to survive a busy week. A short note after a decision, a shared checklist before a release, or a regular review of what is slowing the team down can prevent the same friction from returning.

The point is not to make a process for every situation. It is to notice the repeatable work and make the next pass a little easier. If a system takes more effort to maintain than it saves, it is probably too heavy.

Start with one recurring problem. Make the smallest change that helps, then keep it only if it earns its place. Over time, those small improvements compound into more attention for the work that needs judgment.
