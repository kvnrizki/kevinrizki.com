---
title: What makes a good default?
excerpt: Defaults are tiny decisions with a long reach. They shape how a product feels before anyone changes a thing.
category: Design
date: "2026-09-11"
readingTime: 5
---

A default is a quiet suggestion. It answers a question for someone before they have to ask it, and often becomes the choice they keep.

Good defaults make the common path feel considered without taking away control. They reflect what most people are trying to do, leave room for different needs, and make it easy to understand how to change course.

That makes defaults worth testing with the same care as more visible features. Ask who benefits from the starting point, who has to work around it, and whether the product makes the alternative discoverable.

The smallest setting can carry a big assumption. A good one makes that assumption useful, visible, and easy to revisit.
