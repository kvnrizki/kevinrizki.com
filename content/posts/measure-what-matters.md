---
title: Measure what matters, then look closer
excerpt: Metrics are useful beginnings, not complete answers. Pair the numbers with context and the people behind them.
category: Engineering
date: "2026-08-29"
readingTime: 7
---

Metrics help us notice patterns at a scale memory cannot manage. They can show whether a change made a workflow faster, whether an error is becoming more common, or whether people are finding what they came for.

But a number rarely explains itself. A drop in usage might mean a feature is harder to find, or that people have learned to finish the task another way. The measurement gives us a useful question; it does not always give us the answer.

Pair quantitative signals with context: talk to the people affected, inspect a few real examples, and make the assumptions behind the metric explicit. That turns measurement from a scorecard into a way to learn.

Measure the thing that helps you make a better decision. Then look closer before deciding what the number means.
